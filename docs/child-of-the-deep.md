# The Child of the Deep — Player Origin and Main Narrative
Status: CENTRAL STORY REVISION (proposed 2026-11-11; awaiting story-owner approval)

## Premise
The central game is **not** a stranger stumbling into The Deep; the playable protagonist is an unwilling, unknowing descendant of people expelled from the habitat. The Deep is home, contested inheritance and the decisive political prize. The player can make it an independent sovereign city, a cooperative partner with another faction, a controlled client/protectorate or a force that establishes its own regional ascendancy. These are city-state outcomes layered into the four principal endgame political campaigns; they do not automatically create a fifth campaign with an entirely separate questline.

## Family history and generational timeline
- **2191–2205 War of the Levels** devastated the habitat (provisional historical low around 1,400). Too early to be a two-generation exile for a player aged about 25–40 in 2317.
- **2255–2270, the Exile of the East Galleries** (NEW PROPOSED CRISIS): a political purge after limited recovery forced several hundred residents to the surface through freight and maintenance access. Either one or both of the player's grandparents were among them. Exact date, expulsion size and survival rate to be decided. Descendants settled in the Appalachian valleys, names changed, records sealed and family background concealed.
- Player born on the surface around 2277–2292, grew up with an incomplete but genuine remembered life. Family connection remains unknown; do not erase the player's whole personality/history.
- 2316 the player's investigation as a Meridian Rider reveals evidence of manipulation. A deliberate **targeted memory removal** conceals the last mission and a crucial system key; it does not remove childhood or all memories.
- 2317 the train wreck triggers the game. The player learns the truth progressively.

## Two separate mysteries
1. **Who was my family?** The player follows ancestry through artifacts, testimonies, forged names and maintenance registers. Families were accused of sabotage and treason for challenging resource allocations; the evidence ultimately shows complicated responsibility, not a single immaculate innocence.
2. **What did I discover as a Rider?** A later covert assignment and MERIDIAN intervention. Mara Vale knows more than she first admits. Its connection to The Deep is political and technical, not a hereditary magic power or genetic chosen-one trope.

## Act reframing
**Act I — The Broken Roads / The Exile:** The player survives a rail wreck in the Tellico valley, learns world and gunslinger mechanics, encounters families, abandoned roads, trading settlements and frontier political rivalry. Repeated clues connect to the Deep; Mara at end reveals the player's family once lived below. The player can disbelieve or refuse emotional identification.
**Act II — The Hidden Kingdoms / The Inheritance:** The Deep is the centerpiece, not just a tour stop. The player navigates government, disgraced family stories, sealed districts, records, insurgent descendants, scarce infrastructure and contact with other powers. Exploring Oak Ridge, Hollowborn territories, West Tek parallels and Appalachian connections supplies context. MERIDIAN starts applying pressure; player must decide who deserves trust.
**Act III — The Last Meridian / The Return:** The Deep's allegiance and constitution become the center of bargaining among Free Compact, People's Commonwealth, Iron Dominion and Ascendant Directorate. Player decides whether it joins, partners while preserving self-rule, remains sealed, or rises as a sovereign technological-industrial power with regional influence. All main routes confront MERIDIAN Prime; no optional Deep side quest should soft-lock MQ progression.

## Deep end states / not fifth joinable core faction
A. **Free city**: Independent, constitutional, trades with external groups.
B. **Autonomous ally**: Supports one principal faction by treaty and maintains its own institutions; the player chooses obligations, safeguards and ability to exit.
C. **Integrated**: Joins a principal faction as a member-city, with different autonomy by government type.
D. **Protectorate/occupied**: Under military or technical coercion; possible resistance.
E. **Deep Ascendant**: The Deep itself becomes a regional leader or coalition organizer and can outgrow the chosen faction's ambitions, using independently maintained infrastructure and possibly MERIDIAN technology. To avoid a fifth faction campaign, implement as a high-autonomy late-game political ending variation attainable through a path that allies with and then constrains or supersedes a principal faction. Avoid automatically equating this with Kess's Ascendant Directorate. Distinct possible name e.g. **The Deep Restoration**; final title undecided.
F. **Sealed city**: Refuses surface contact, survival consequences.
G. **Fractured**: Internal schism and partial evacuation, not automatic total genocide.

## NPC political roles
**Helena Sorrell**: Steward head, records custodian and prior regime representative; may know purge details.
**Imani Cross**: Reform advocate; challenges records and advocates transparency.
**Marcus Thorne**: Collective integration advocate, not necessarily pure villain.
**Lena Orr**: Archives/forensic historian who reveals census discrepancies.
**Mara Vale**: Ex-Rider intermediary, capable of betrayal or confession.

## Quest beats
- Early recurring emblem/torn transit pass on family belongings and on old rail goods.
- Act I MQ05 The Fifth Rider: Mara discloses only that the player's family came from the habitat.
- Act II MQ06 A Thousand Floors: protagonist denied lawful entry at family-record terminal; investigation into erased citizens.
- Optional linked family quest **The Names They Took**: trace alias chain through Mercy Crossing, river refugees and East Gallery rosters.
- Quest **The East Galleries**: closed neighborhood reveals purge evidence, surviving witnesses and rival testimony about sabotage.
- MQ11 The Unwritten History: recover 2316 Rider mission memory separately from the ancestral records.
- Act III MQ15 The Gathering: Deep institutional choice/partnership decisive in alliance calculus, but player's faction path still runs.
- MQ17 The Last Decision: Deep's constitution and degree of sovereignty are explicit epilogue variables.

## Rules
- Do not define player ethnicity, sex, appearance, moral character, childhood personality or exact parents; preserve customizable character and player role-play.
- Make chronology genuinely two generations (mid-23rd-century exile), separate from earlier War of the Levels.
- No preordained inherited powers. Family records may grant political standing or access evidence, but legitimacy must come through player choices.
- Major decisions should be actions with gameplay consequences, not single dialogue toggles.
- Introduce third parties via artifacts, visitors, sparse Brotherhood traces and travel, not a fifth major political system.
