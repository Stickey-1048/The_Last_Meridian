# History, Places and Hollowborn
Status: Working in-universe fiction. Do not confuse these events with Bethesda canon or verified real-life Oak Ridge history.

## Timeline
- 1940s: Real Manhattan Project establishes Oak Ridge as a center of nuclear research; **fictional** classified researchers encounter anomalous biological samples or unknown human lineages. Discovery mechanism is NOT established.
- 2060s–2077: **fictional** Meridian continuity initiative grows within secret infrastructure research; later laboratories investigate anomalous signal phenomena. Timeline of advanced genetic research remains provisional.
- 2077-10-23: Fallout Great War.
- 2078–2150: Underground survivors develop institutions; surface communities emerge around water, farms and transport.
- 2150–2300: Meridian Riders monitor/intervene; experimental subjects and descendant communities become independent. Specific dates remain provisional until Fallout 76 overlap audit.
- 2316: Fifth Rider discovers MERIDIAN manipulation and self-erases memories.
- 2317: Player wakes in train wreck; main story begins.

## Regional topology
**Broken Valleys**: Tellico Plains/Tellico River inspiration, Mercy Crossing and farms.
**Iron Corridor**: Loudon/Lenoir City inspiration; Iron Junction, old industrial approaches to Oak Ridge.
**Shrouded Divide**: Great Smokies/Nantahala inspiration; Hollow Crown, Blackwater Gap and Lantern Hollow.
**Cinder Highlands**: North Georgia Blue Ridge inspiration; Cinder Station and mountain clans.
**Oak Ridge Exclusion**: Secret fictional installations; the real geography is inspiration, not a real claim.
**The Deep** and **Meridian Prime**: separate underground worldspaces in eastern Tennessee.
Connections: old highways, tunnels, trestles, reservoirs, ferries and barges; no western desert.

## Hollowborn = umbrella term
Recognizably human biology, subtle heritable features; NOT an entirely separate fantasy race.
- Hartfolk: sensory acuity and rare small cranial growths, Hollow Crown.
- Mirekin: unusual aquatic adaptations and pigmentation, Blackwater Gap.
- Ursans: larger, denser human physiques, Cinder Ridge.
- Nocturnes: altered low-light perception/hearing, Lantern Hollow.
- Vulpers: unusual sensory traits, courier settlements.
- Glassborn: subtle patterned markings and strange neurological responses, Glass Orchard.
- Eldren: long-lived traditions, unusual perception (exact physiology uncertain).
- Stonebound: underground engineering houses, human-derived, culturally distinct; avoid simplistic dwarf caricatures.
- Forged: later military engineered lineage (origin provisional).

## Competing theories
1. A multi-generational Manhattan Project/Oak Ridge offshoot caused some traits.
2. Researchers found preexisting unusual lineages and exploited them.
3. Anomalous signals influenced later biology/technology.
Different lineages may have different origins. Do not resolve all in one exposition dump. No real-world claim that such populations or experiments existed.

## Nuclear train
Iron Saint is a rare surviving experimental atomic locomotive, not a mass-produced replacement for diesel; needs credible reactor shielding, traction, fuel maintenance and track repair. Horse survival is an open lore question. Fusion buggies must be rare salvaged technology.
