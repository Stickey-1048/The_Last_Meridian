# External Fallout Canon: Southern Appalachian Encounters
Status: PROPOSED narrative integration, not Bethesda-canon additions.
Last researched: 2026-11-11. No main-faction replacement or new required story spine.

## Story design rule
The game is set in southern Appalachia (eastern TN / western NC / northern GA) in 2317. Recognize the wider Fallout universe through plausible limited travelers, caches, dead expeditions, radios, records, equipment, refugees and trade. Do **not** assert that any canonical protagonist or established faction leaders personally appear in these areas, nor fix player-dependent choices from the original games. No foreign power becomes a fifth principal faction.

- Fallout 76: Fort Atlas, Paladin Leila Rahmani, Knight Shin, Scribe Valdez, and the ideological split (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/fallout-76-steel-dawn-update-notes-november-24-2020; https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/steel-reign-available-now).
- Fallout 76: Blue Ridge Caravan Company, mountain freight and Big Bend Tunnel (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/fallout-76-inside-the-vault-gear-up-for-riding-shotgun-and-radiation-rumble).
- Fallout 76: Dr. Blackburn and colleagues' FEV strain work in a secret laboratory beneath West Tek Research Center, as described by Bethesda quest support (https://help.bethesda.net/app/answers/detail/a_id/53622/).
- Fallout 3: Vault 101 and the Capital Wasteland, Brotherhood/Enclave conflict, super mutants, and Broken Steel's Enclave remnants and Liberty Prime (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en/games/fallout-3; https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-NZ/news/where-to-wander-a-guide-to-fallout-games).
- Fallout 76: as of Sept 2026 Bethesda previewed a future Raven Rock/Enclave map expansion. NOT yet settled story canon; avoid any statements about its final plot outcomes (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-EU/news/letter-from-the-directors-september-2026).

## Recommended encounter / quest register
All entries below are *new invented Last Meridian content*, to be distinguished from published lore.

### X01 The Unanswered Patrol (Brotherhood; Shrouded Divide)
Explore a collapsed monitoring station at a mountain pass. Decades or centuries earlier, an unnamed Brotherhood scouting party searched for unusual signal traffic, lost long-distance radio, and disagreed whether to withdraw. Find one rusted power armor frame and incomplete armor pieces, handwritten field orders and three conflicting logs. Survivors' final fate unclear; possible isolated descendant NPC with no claim to represent all Brotherhood. Branch: repair the local relay to learn the fate of a patrol, sell salvage, or return it to a claimant. Avoid preselecting whether Rahmani or Shin led the wider 76 Brotherhood decades later.

### X02 The Armor Below (equipment; Iron Corridor)
One stripped, corroded set of T-series or another verified existing Fallout armor model is found locked inside a collapsed pre-war maintenance structure. Parts scavenged by miners; fusion core dead or missing. Repair needs plausible rare pieces. A unique historical marker, not an armory worth dozens of suits. Never make power armor ubiquitous in the Western opening.

### X03 Blue Ridge Returns (travelers; Mercy Crossing)
An aging caravan log from the WV Blue Ridge network is recovered from a freight cache; a modern independent trader claims family ties to the company. Accept ambiguous claims rather than certify current corporate survival through 2317. Contract: trace a lost river/rail handoff, compare payment records, settle ownership and reopening rights.

### X04 Northern Voices (Capital Wasteland rumors; roadside radio)
Recover a radio receiver capable of intermittent long-distance reception (repeater-assisted, not omniscient). Elderly travelers trade stories of the D.C. ruins, the Brotherhood and the Enclave; a pre-recorded recovered broadcast mentions Project Purity or the conflict around it. Do not canonize the Lone Wanderer's gender, ending-specific choices or later outcomes. Realistic reception and chronology important: do not treat century-old live transmissions as contemporary.

### X05 The West Tek File (Oak Ridge archives; FEV context)
Oak Ridge researchers stored fragmentary public/scientific intelligence indicating West Tek FEV work and mutation hazards. The Last Meridian **Parallel Adaptation Program (PAP)** is distinct: studied inherited human adaptations, radioprotection, longevity and ecosystem survivability; its failures were unethical and uncertain, and need not produce super mutants. Some scientists attempted to replicate fragments of FEV science after the war, but do NOT make PAP a renamed West Tek program or imply FEV automatically causes the Hollowborn. Player can disclose findings, prevent dangerous replication, or preserve records for ethical study.

### X06 The Ashes of the Grid (TVA hydropower; Tellico/Little Tennessee)
Repair a controllable hydro station or gate system. The player disputes power dispatch, flood management and river navigation with downstream towns. Use old TVA operating logs, switchgear, transformers and water infrastructure. Depict fictional postwar facility failures, not precise real-world target or attack claims.

### X07 Eastbound, No Return (Asheville/Charlotte; eastern rumor)
A trader brings a map annotated with alternative eastern routes through Asheville, foothill towns and the Charlotte region. The player never needs to enter Charlotte. Accounts conflict: some urban industrial pockets survived, others were devastated by war and infrastructure collapse. The map's old nuclear plant markings identify facilities near Charlotte and in the wider Carolinas, not a supposed Asheville reactor. Preserve uncertainty on 2077 targeting, releases and population.

### X08 An Engineer's Last Report (Old-world grid; Oak Ridge/river)
A dead civilian utility engineer's terminal describes alternating lines of survival: mountain valleys avoided immediate blast zones, but suffered smoke, famine, weather, contamination carried by water or wind, and grid loss. Explicitly avoid claims that mountain ridges block all fallout or that every nuclear power plant necessarily melted down after the war.

## Regional world state and backdrop
- **West Virginia route**: rumors of 76-era Responders, Settlers, Raiders, Brotherhood, Enclave, Scorched and Blue Ridge, with centuries of changing hearsay; the player needn't meet any iconic canon character.
- **D.C. route**: traders via Virginia corridor, possibly along segments of pre-war road/rail; older records from Fallout 3 period, not direct physical proximity.
- **Asheville**: plausible separate eastern trade hub or damaged city; no official Bethesda status established.
- **Charlotte**: remote eastern commercial/political rumor city; real-world reactor facilities at McGuire (Huntersville, NC) and Catawba (York, SC); no Bethesda-confirmed strike/destruction fate.
- **TVA**: postwar grid ruins and surviving hydropower infrastructure; modern real-world nuclear plants Watts Bar near Spring City, Sequoyah near Soddy-Daisy, Browns Ferry near Athens AL, should not be relocated on maps.
- **Oak Ridge**: historically real Manhattan Project legacy plus separately invented in-universe research. The Parallel Adaptation Program is not historical fact.

## Fallout/production guardrails
1. Canon lore references should be optional and add texture, not create mandatory quest gates.
2. No canonical character voices/likenesses portrayed as officially licensed without rights review.
3. No guaranteed 2077 nuclear attack on a specific real plant absent official canon; fictional war scenarios are flagged speculative.
4. Keep the player-centered setting isolated by impassable mountain roads and old infrastructure, not by asserting no one else in the East survived.
5. The Deep's history and MERIDIAN must be distinct from Vault-Tec, MODUS and West Tek experiments.
6. Historical expedition dates, radio logs, power armor variant and technology will be reconciled with published game timeline during quest scripting.
