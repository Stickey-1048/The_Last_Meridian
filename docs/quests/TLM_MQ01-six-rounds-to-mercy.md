## 1. Scope

In scope: one quest record, `TLM_MQ01`, covering the rail wreck, the sealed dispatch, the road holdup of a caravan, arrival at Mercy Crossing and the first conversation with Sheriff Owen Pritchard; plus one optional evidence thread about a falsified track-maintenance ledger ("the section book").

Out of scope, deliberately:
- Any late-story faction, The Deep, Mara Vale, the Riders, memory recovery.
- Why the player was on the train, who the player's family is, or any Deep clue. The emblem / torn transit pass beat in docs/child-of-the-deep.md is reserved as an empty prop slot at the wreck and is **not** implemented until that document is approved.
- Full player lines. Player responses are given as wheel prompts and intent only (CLAUDE.md narrative note).
- Total-conversion new-game bootstrap, character creation, voiced dialogue, final art.
- Papyrus source files. Section 11 assigns responsibilities per script; code is written after the quest, aliases and conditions exist in the editor (docs/development-plan.md, docs/doctrine-of-the-open-ledger.md).

Design rules this quest follows:
- The player can **help, evade or exploit** the caravan [CANON], and none of the three blocks completion.
- Ideology shows up as consequences (cargo, stock, who keeps their job, what the sheriff can act on), never as a required player speech.
- Scarcity is tracked physically (a supply counter and vendor stock), not as a moral score (docs/doctrine-of-the-open-ledger.md).
- The quest must not establish what caused the wreck. Act I's sabotage plot stays open.

## 7. Caravan decision state

Situation [NEW]: at the ford, three road agents have stopped Hattie Lowell's inbound caravan (Lowell, one guard, one pack brahmin, one cargo crate) and are demanding a toll she cannot pay. Guns are out; nobody has fired. The standoff holds until the player acts or leaves. There is no timer in v0.1.

### Outcomes

| Value | Name | Reached when | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unresolved | Default | |
| 1 | Defended | All road agents dead and Lowell alive | Help |
| 2 | Brokered | Speech challenge succeeds, or the player pays the toll; nobody shot | Help |
| 3 | Bypassed | Outcome still 0 when the player reaches Mercy Crossing | Evade |
| 4 | Sided with the agents | Player accepts the boss's offer of a cut | Exploit |
| 5 | Caravan dead | Lowell dies before the caravan reaches town, by anyone's hand | Exploit if 216 or 218 is set; otherwise a failed defence |

Rules:
1. **Single writer.** Only `ResolveCaravan(outcome)` writes `TLM_MQ01_CaravanOutcome`, `TLM_MQ01_CargoDelivered`, stages 250–255 and the reputation change.
2. **Latched.** The first resolution stands, with one override: Lowell's death before stage 260 changes any outcome to 5.
3. **One evade rule.** "Outcome is still 0 at stage 400" covers every way of not engaging: never seeing the holdup, watching and leaving, going overland.
4. Demanding pay (212) is an exploit flavour of outcomes 1 and 2, not a separate outcome.

### Consequences

| Outcome | Cargo delivered | `TLM_MC_Supply` | `TLM_Rep_FreeCompact` | Player gets | World state at Mercy Crossing | Agents |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Defended | 1 | +1 | +1; 0 if 212 | Six .44 rounds and 40 caps from Lowell; with 212, 100 caps and no rounds [TUNE] | Lowell, guard if alive, and brahmin at the trading post; `TLM_MQ01_CaravanStock` added to the vendor chest | Dead |
| 2 Brokered | 1 | +1 | +1; 0 if 212 | Same as 1; minus 25 caps if 214 | Same as 1 | Alive; `TLM_RoadAgentsAtLarge` = 1 |
| 3 Bypassed | 0 | 0 | 0 | Nothing | Lowell arrives on foot, robbed; no new stock | Alive, at large |
| 4 Sided | 0 | 0 | −1 | 60 caps and two items from the crate [TUNE] | Same as 3, and Lowell knows the player's face | Alive, at large |
| 5 Caravan dead | 0 | 0 | −1 if 218; else 0 | Whatever the player loots (sets 216) | Nobody arrives; no new stock | Depends on the fight |

The costs sit on every path: fighting spends the six rounds and risks Lowell; brokering leaves armed men on the road; walking past keeps the player whole and the town short; exploiting pays now and closes a door with the Compact's traders. Nobody lectures the player about any of it. The debrief only changes which facts Pritchard has heard.

Deferred refinement: making the reputation penalty depend on a surviving witness. It fits the accountability theme but doubles the state to test.

## 8. Secondary objective: the section book

Working title "The Section Book" [NEW]. Optional. Invisible until the player touches a piece of evidence.

### Fiction [NEW, original; nothing here is Bethesda canon]

Track into the valley is kept by section gangs. Each gang keeps a section book: inspections, ties renewed, spikes drawn from stores, gauge readings, signed by the foreman and countersigned when stores issues material. Pay and rations for the gang are issued against the work the book records.

The book in the shanty beside the wreck says that a week ago the gang renewed forty ties at this mile, drove two kegs of spikes and found the gauge true. Signed "W. Garrity, Section Foreman, Mercy Crossing". The countersignature is a smudge.

What is physically there:
- **The rail** at the derailment point sits on grey, split ties. No fresh timber. Old spikes, and several holes with no spike at all.
- **The two kegs** are in the shanty, still wired shut, with stores tags carrying the same date as the book entry.

So the work was signed for and not done. Garrity's account, if the player gets it [TEMP summary]: his gang was cut to two men; no logged work meant no rations for them; he signed, meaning to do the job the following week. The empty spike holes are consistent with neglect and equally with someone pulling spikes. **MQ01 does not say which.** The thread proves a false record, not the cause of the wreck.

This is the Act I "symptom" called for by docs/doctrine-of-the-open-ledger.md: a small lie in a ledger, told for an understandable reason, under a rule nobody in the room wrote. It also asks the player to meet the standard they hold others to: a book alone proves nothing.

### Evidence model

| Piece | Record | How obtained | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 Section book | `SectionBook` alias | Taken from the shanty | 310 |
| E2 Rail | `RailEvidence` activator | "Inspect"; shows `TLM_MQ01_RailInspectMsg` | 320 |
| E3 Kegs | `KegEvidence` activator | "Examine"; shows `TLM_MQ01_KegInspectMsg` | 325 |

Evidence strength = number of physical checks done (0, 1 or 2). It is computed from stages 320 and 325 whenever needed and never stored.

- Any piece touched opens the thread (300).
- Book plus strength 1 or more is **corroborated** (330).
- Order does not matter. Inspecting the rail first shows objective 300 pointing at the shanty.
- The message text states what is seen and stops. It draws no conclusion for the player.

### Outcomes

| Value | Name | Reached when |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Not found | Book never taken |
| 1 | Withheld | Book taken, nothing done with it by stage 1000. Player keeps the book |
| 2 | Reported, unproven | Book given to Pritchard at strength 0 |
| 3 | Reported, proven | Book given to Pritchard at strength 1 or 2 |
| 4 | Foreman reported himself | Garrity admitted it and the player told him to go to the sheriff |
| 5 | Sold | Player took Garrity's money for the book |

Single writer: only `ResolveLedger(outcome)` writes `TLM_MQ01_LedgerOutcome`, stages 350–355, the metrics and `TLM_MC_SectionCrewActive`. Latched on first call.

### Consequences

| Outcome | What changes in the world | Metrics (placeholder) | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Not found | Nothing | none | |
| 1 Withheld | Nothing. The player holds a document someone would pay for | none | Nothing gets fixed |
| 2 Unproven | Pritchard keeps the book and tells the player plainly he cannot act on a signature alone | `TLM_Truth` +1 | The book is out of the player's hands and nothing happens |
| 3 Proven | Pritchard holds Garrity for questions and will ride out to check the track himself. `TLM_MC_SectionCrewActive` = 0. Sets hook `TLM_MQ01_StoresLead` = 1: the inquiry will ask who countersigned | `TLM_Truth` +1, `TLM_Accountability` +1 | The valley's only section gang stops work |
| 4 Self-reported | Garrity walks to the sheriff's office and makes a statement; he keeps working under Pritchard's eye. `TLM_MC_SectionCrewActive` = 1. No stores lead: he names nobody else | `TLM_Truth` +1, `TLM_Accountability` +1, `TLM_MutualTrust` +1 | The countersigner is never asked about |
| 5 Sold | Player +60 caps [TUNE]; book gone; Garrity keeps working | none | The record stays false |

Pritchard checking the track himself rather than taking the player's word is deliberate: independent review is the second point of the Open Ledger Charter.

`TLM_MQ01_StoresLead`, `TLM_MC_SectionCrewActive` and `TLM_RoadAgentsAtLarge` are hooks for quests that do not exist yet. They are written and never read in the slice.

### Why this lives inside MQ01 and ends with it

One quest record is easier to build and test first. The price is that the thread closes when the dispatch conversation ends: a player who talks to the sheriff before meeting Garrity can report or withhold but can no longer confront him. Withheld is a real, readable outcome, so nothing is lost for later quests. If playtest shows players losing the thread, move stages 300–355 into their own quest record without changing the logic.

## 9. Scenes and dialogue

Player entries are wheel prompts with intent. Full player lines are deliberately not written. NPC lines are [TEMP]. Speech challenges are Medium [TUNE]. Slot names follow Fallout 4's four-way player dialogue (section 17, F22).

Two editor mechanisms used below could not be confirmed from documentation and must be proven in the editor before features 5 and 6 are estimated: starting a scene from a greeting (F23) and authoring a speech challenge with separate success and failure responses (F24). If the speech challenge cannot be authored as assumed, replace each one with a plain condition on a player stat [TUNE]; the stage logic does not change.

## 11. Papyrus responsibilities

No code yet. One quest script holds every state transition; alias scripts only report events to it. Bethesda's default scripts are used where one fits (section 17, F1–F5); otherwise the small custom script named here.

**`TLM:MQ01_QuestScript` (on the quest)**

| Function | Does |
|---|---|
| `ResolveCaravan(int outcome)` | Enforces latch and override. Writes outcome global, cargo global, stages 250 and 25x, reputation, `TLM_RoadAgentsAtLarge`. Stops the standoff scene. Disables the cargo crate when someone carries it off (outcomes 1–4). Re-evaluates packages |
| `EnsureCaravanResolved()` | If outcome is 0, calls `ResolveCaravan(3)` |
| `DoTownHandoff()` | Once only (260). Moves or enables caravan actors by outcome, adds `TLM_MQ01_CaravanStock` to the vendor chest and raises `TLM_MC_Supply` when cargo is delivered, enables the aftermath marker for outcomes 3 and 4 |
| `BeginHostilities()` | Stage 220 body: faction flip, clear Lowell's protection, stop scenes |
| `UpdateLedgerEvidence()` | Called at 310, 320, 325. Sets 300, sets 330 when corroborated, moves objectives |
| `int GetEvidenceStrength()` | Counts stages 320 and 325 |
| `ResolveLedger(int outcome)` | Latched. Writes outcome global, stages 350 and 35x, metrics, crew and lead globals, moves the book or caps, re-evaluates the foreman |
| `CloseLedgerThread()` | At 1000. If 310 done and 350 not done, `ResolveLedger(1)`. Hides any open thread objective |
| `RepairState()` | Idempotent. Rebuilds objective display from stages, recreates the dispatch in the satchel if it is neither delivered nor findable, returns the sheriff to his office if he is disabled or missing, calls `EnsureCaravanResolved()` if 400 is done |

|---|---|
| Save and reload at any stage | State is stages and globals only. `RepairState()` runs on load and re-derives objectives |
