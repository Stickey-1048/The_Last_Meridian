# Fallout Lore Compatibility Audit
Status: OPEN / NOT YET VERIFIED AGAINST PRIMARY REFERENCES
Goal: compatible fan-fiction continuity, NOT an assertion of official Bethesda canon.

## Evidence protocol
For each lore-sensitive assertion log: claim; game/source; exact location or quest; source link/quote where permissible; date/era; compatibility status (Compatible / Needs explanation / Conflicts / Unknown); resolution. Favor official game dialogue, terminals and Bethesda materials over fan assumptions. Recheck current canon before locking major claims.

| Topic | Risk | Proposed handling | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Great War | High | Keep date Oct 23, 2077, fixed. | Compatible baseline |
| Fallout 76 Appalachia | High | Distinguish game regions, groups and events from 2102 onward; account for later canon and changed geography by 2317. | Research required |
| Fallout 4 lore | Medium | Retain radiation, ghouls, robots, fusion, power armor, timeline; do not contradict known East Coast continuity. | Research required |

| Hollowborn | Medium | Original human-derived populations. No claim of an established official species. | Original lore |
| Distant Signal | High | Novel, ambiguous phenomenon; **does not cause/explain Great War**. | Original lore |
| MERIDIAN | High | New continuity infrastructure; clearly separate from Vault-Tec, the Institute, Enclave, and known AI. | Original lore |
| Fallout rights & assets | High | Follow Bethesda mod terms and attribution; no commercial claims or copyrighted third-party franchise designs. | Legal review required |

## Research tasks
1. Fallout 76 location/quest chronology overlapping TN, NC, northern GA; do not assume these states are already depicted.
2. Fallout timeline through 2317, including major East Coast events and factions.
3. In-game precedent for genetic modification, FEV, radiation and inherited traits.
4. Ghoul physiology and longevity limitations; remember variable outcomes.
5. Operational plausibility and canon precedents for trains, automobiles and horses.
6. Bethesda Creation Kit policies and redistributed asset restrictions.

## Rule
Do not label an assertion 'verified' without recording its primary reference. Where sources conflict, document conflicts and choose the least disruptive invented addition.
