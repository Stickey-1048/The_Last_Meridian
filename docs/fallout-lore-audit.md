## Publisher-confirmed cross-check (November 11, 2026)
Official Bethesda pages establish:
- **Fallout 76 begins in 2102, 25 years after the Great War** (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/fallout-76-official-reveal-e3-2018).
- **Wastelanders takes place in 2103**, with Foundation settlers and Crater raiders; do not erase their existence in contemporary 2103 background (https://fallout.bethesda.net/zh-CN/news/fallout-76-inside-the-vault-wastelanders-preview-panel-recap-and-gameplay).
- **The Appalachian Brotherhood has a documented Rahmani/Shin conflict** during Steel Dawn/Steel Reign; don't assign their canonical outcomes universally to the entire region (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/steel-reign-available-now).
- **Blue Ridge Caravan Company and Big Bend Tunnel** are established trade-route and caravan lore (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/fallout-76-inside-the-vault-gear-up-for-riding-shotgun-and-radiation-rumble; https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/fallout-76-milepost-zero-release-notes).
- **Skyline Valley is in Shenandoah (Virginia), not the Tennessee/NC Smokies**, and features Vault 63, the Lost, and Hugo Stolz. Distinguish The Deep's ordinary engineering/vertical habitat and its politics from Vault 63's experiment and electrically altered ghouls (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/fallout-76-skyline-valley-available-now).
- **Burning Springs is southeastern Ohio**, and includes Highway Town, the Rust King and bounty hunting; do not incorporate it as a Tennessee location (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/fallout-76-burning-springs-release-notes).
- Bethesda's official expansion overview confirms the key regional factions and histories (https://fallout.bethesda.net/en/expansions).

### Compatibility conclusions
**Low current geographic collision:** The game's main exterior maps are in eastern TN, western NC and northern GA, outside the principal Bethesda-described 76 WV/Virginia/Ohio game regions. This is NOT a guarantee against later content updates or lore references.
**Historical continuity:** Do not claim southern Appalachia is the whole of Appalachia, or that all survivors were confined underground for 240 years. Independent communities can coexist with contemporaneous developments in WV. Cross-border traders might have heard of Responders, Foundation, the Crater, the Brotherhood, the Enclave and Blue Ridge Company—absence or fragmentary knowledge is plausible, but universal historical erasure is not.
**No invented official link:** Do not make Meridian parent agency of Vault 63, MODUS/Enclave, or the Scorched Plague; if any references occur, treat them as externally known phenomena, not causes of the game's plot.
**Design caution: horses:** Canon status of widespread surviving horse populations remains unsettled. Avoid making horse mounts critical to the main quest until an official-text audit; brahmin pack animals are secure default.
**Design caution: mutations:** Hollowborn are an original human-derived lineage. Fallout includes mutation and experiments, but exact hereditary mechanisms and relation to FEV require original research evidence; do not assert official endorsement.
**Remaining high-priority checks:** Fallout 76 newest updates; in-game terminals on ghoul, FEV, robotic and bunker research; precise southern Appalachian place naming, topography and rail infrastructure; Fallout 4 time 2287 and later events; mod packaging permissions.
