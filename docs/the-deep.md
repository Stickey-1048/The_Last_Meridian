# The Deep — Vertical Continuity Habitat
Status: PROVISIONAL design revision requested 2026-11-11. Authoritative direction: industrial Silo-meets-Fallout vault, not futuristic megacity.

## Architectural identity
Fictional Appalachian Continuity Habitat 03 beneath eastern Tennessee, with Oak Ridge-adjacent industrial/rail service connections. A single enormous vertical concrete core/shaft with stacked working and residential levels, heavy freight elevators, emergency staircases, utilitarian catwalks, mechanical ventilation, repairable piping, analog equipment, terminals, relay cabinets and grimy communal spaces. Earlier sketch proposed 60–80 principal levels; **SUPERSEDED / NOT CANON:** prefer a smaller, game-feasible number of mixed-use levels organized into 4–6-level neighborhood clusters, with distributed kitchens, baths, workshops, ventilation and backup utilities, supported by citywide plants and lateral excavation. Exact floor count remains open. Some whole floors are deserted, sealed or repurposed. No sleek hovering transports, luminous utopian towers or inexplicably advanced fabrication.

## Population
Original sealed occupancy about **8,000 in 2077**. Provisional all-systems capacity ~12,000; not continuous population. Population has expanded and collapsed repeatedly from revolution, rationing failure, epidemics and industrial disaster. Historical low ~1,400 after War of the Levels; present ~6,800 in 2317. THESE FIGURES ARE STORY DESIGN PROPOSALS; technical feasibility of agriculture, energy, water and reproduction needs audit.

## Crises
- 2152–2165 **Ration Riots**: crop failures, corrupt food distribution; backlash against administrators.
- 2191–2205 **War of the Levels**: factions hold floor blocs; elevators, water and ventilation weaponized; many habitable floors abandoned; lowest population ~1,400.
- 2260–2285 **Second Opening**: surface contact and information reform pursued, partial recovery and contested openness.

## Story and politics
The Deep is **NOT automatically a fifth principal endgame faction**, but it is the story's ANCESTRAL FOCUS. The player is descended from a family expelled in a later purge, and can guide The Deep toward sovereign dominance as a distinct ending variation without necessarily creating a fifth separate campaign. It is a sovereign, consequential city-state and possible strengthening ally to one of the four main powers (Free Meridian Compact, People's Commonwealth, Iron Dominion, Ascendant Directorate), or an independent trading city / sealed sovereign habitat. Factional support can be a voluntary industrial treaty rather than annexation. Collapse/fracture is a possible local outcome.

Possible internal movements:
- **Stewards** led by Helena Sorrell: stability/continuity, withholding surface truth.
- **Open Door** led by Imani Cross: transparency, elected representation and trade.
- **Common Purpose** led by Marcus Thorne: collective ownership and Commonwealth integration.
- **Quiet Ledger** led by archivist Lena Orr: forensic records and MERIDIAN corruption.

These are INTERNAL POLITICAL GROUPS, not new top-level factions. Their standing, and which movement originally founded The Deep, remain provisional until history is reconciled with The Deep's earlier Continuity Directorate and the four-faction alignment.

## Key gameplay areas
- Entrance freight lock and medical quarantine.
- Central lift/stair shaft and ring corridors.
- Live residential levels and communal canteens.
- Workshops, food production and clinic.
- Abandoned living levels used for environmental storytelling and exploration.
- Sealed sectors dating to War of the Levels, including independent survivor holdouts.
- Lower pump rooms, generation/steam utilities and mechanical control rooms.
- Records vault with falsified survival reports.

## Continuity and production cautions
No claim of a real Manhattan Project habitat. Keep architecture distinct from direct copying of Silo series proprietary designs. Design believable utilities. Do not automatically combine The Deep and People's Commonwealth. Act II still progresses if player does not complete independent Deep political quests.
