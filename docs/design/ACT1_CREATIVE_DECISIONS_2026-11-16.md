## The five governing decisions

1. **One purse, independent hands. Yes.** Blackstone silver reaches Silas Vane and funds the rail, river, mine and Cutter operations. Each participant retains a personal motive, incomplete knowledge and responsibility. Vane finances isolation and obstruction; he does not control every faction, cause every accident or know the hidden explanation. Exposing, pressuring and recruiting him remain distinct possibilities. His useful donations do not erase his victims.
2. **Ostrander lives; Lockridge forged the inspections. Yes.** Reuben Ostrander and Ezekiel "Preacher" Dole are one person. Ostrander was reported/presumed dead after the fever; Gussie Lockridge used his stamp to sign unsafe equipment clean. Chapter 1's wreck results from neglected equipment and fraudulent certification. Dole's later deliberate rail attacks are separate crimes. Corroborate the later identification and forgery with the stamp, records and testimony. Chapter 1 identifies contradictions and fraud without identifying its author; its reported death is evidence available to Gantt, not an omniscient fact.
3. **One early Stonebound meeting. Yes, bounded.** Helga Dorn may be encountered at a sealed working/gallery below Red Hollow. She represents her household or House, not every underground community. The player can rescue miners and carry terms, and can make a personal temporary undertaking; the player cannot bind a whole town by saying one sentence. No entry into The Deep, lineage/origin answer, ancestral recognition or required ancillary allegiance follows. Chapter 9 retains the substantial cultural and political encounter. Do not call this the first meeting any surface person has ever had with a Stonebound person.
4. **Voss at the fair: choose the distant version.** Keep the roof shot, distant escape evidence and silver case. Remove the face-to-face, unkillable conversational cameo and the guaranteed-futile catch/kill objective. Investigating the firing position, helping the courier and confronting an opportunistic occupation remain meaningful alternatives. No name, subtitle or marker reveals the assassin. A later close encounter requires a design that can honor player interference.
5. **Defer the recognition hints.** Remove/rework the four recognition nodes `voss_line`, `voss_know`, `flint_line`, `flint_hat`; remove the memory/recognition endings of `grey_offer` and `grey_train`. Remove the other face-to-face Voss dialogue made impossible by decision 4, and corresponding pre-reveal recognition assertions in Grey/Voss/Flint character metadata. Praise may concern a shot the player actually made, not a predetermined teacher or childhood. Childhood, parents, ethnicity, appearance and moral history remain player-controlled/unsettled as already specified. Only the recent 2316 mission memories were erased. Mara's Chapter 5 entrance and the existing reveal boundary remain intact.
