# Act I storyboard and scene development

Status: PROPOSED narrative development, 2026-11-14. Based on graybox commit 32f592677055363c5d63c7d27d33443b44100077. New scenes, motives, objects, roles and consequences below require narrative review. Existing quest behavior is unchanged.

The Broken Roads follows a stranger whose practical choices acquire a public history before anyone knows their private history. The opening asks whether a person will intervene. The middle asks what their intervention actually achieved. The end widens the mystery without deciding who the player is entitled to become.

Read alongside [character and dialogue development](ACT1_character_and_dialogue_development.md) and [research and editorial review](ACT1_research_and_editorial_review.md). These are development proposals for the story owner and the engine developer, not replacement quest specifications.

## Scope and continuity

Three levels are supplied: chapter storyboard; playable scenes with choices and consequences; and character performance, dialogue and objects in the companion document. Chapters 1 to 4 receive substantive development. Chapter 5 receives a location and encounter framework only: genealogy, Mara's exact involvement and memory revelations remain subject to the unresolved choices in child-of-the-deep.md.

Preserve the real childhood and customizable identity of the wanderer. No spoken player lines are drafted. Bracketed actions and topic labels are interface intentions, not speech. Silence never automatically means consent, guilt, forgiveness or loyalty.

The setting remains wet southern Appalachia in 2317. Four principal endgame factions remain. Ordinary people in the opening know freight, fever, wages and washed-out roads; they do not explain the underground plot. Corwin Haskell remains innocent. Vane's culpability remains unresolved in Chapter 1 and becomes examinable in Chapter 4. Optional evidence and companion arcs never gate the main route.

## Level one chapter storyboard

| Chapter | Dramatic movement | Player's practical question | Image that carries the change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Six Rounds to Mercy | A survivor becomes someone the town has heard about | What can I carry out of this wreck, and who do I stop for? | An intact dispatch seal beside a broken coupling; later, a space at the clinic occupied or empty according to events |
| 2 Three Roads to Ruin | Three apparently local failures reveal a market for keeping places apart | Who benefits from the delay, and who is paying for it? | Grain sacks waiting beneath a damaged roof while the receipt calls them delivered |
| 3 The Four Flags | Emergency assistance becomes a contest over terms | Whose help can people accept without surrendering the right to refuse? | Four offers laid on a working table; a messenger's dropped case after a shot |
| 4 The Black Ledger | Plausible explanations encounter a traceable chain of payments | What does the evidence establish, and what settlement will I enforce? | Vane correcting a small honest arithmetic error beside a much larger concealed transaction |
| 5 The Fifth Rider | The local mystery reaches an unfamiliar boundary | How do I approach a place where somebody expected me? | A station built for freight with signs of recent use below its public platforms |

The proposed connective tissue is exploitation of different failures, not one mastermind causing every shortage. Vane finances selective obstruction to protect advantageous local trade arrangements. Natural damage, unsafe work and sincere fear remain independently real. The train's forged inspection signature is not automatically proof that Vane ordered the wreck. A separate evidentiary question survives.

## Level two Chapter 1 scenes

### Wreck and first obligations

Begin with a practical route through the debris. The player can find shelter, salvage and a way west without reading everything. Gantt is dead; the dispatch remains recognizable as an obligation someone failed to finish. No internal narration tells the player to honor it.

Proposed staging: wet paper stuck beneath the cab seat; a handhold polished by use; the railway still audible through cooling metal. These suggest work interrupted rather than a display arranged for an investigator. Avoid spectacular debris that obscures the actual interactive clues.

Optional inspection retains the existing distinction: physical wear contradicts the maintenance claim; records establish falsification at the existing evidence threshold; nothing identifies a culprit. Pike's lack of sales proves only a gap in her own supply record. Her dialogue must leave room for other suppliers even where the existing quest combines evidence more broadly.

Exit: the main road remains reachable with the dispatch, without the revolver, and without Signed Clean.

### Wagon at the narrowing

Keep the implemented hold-up rather than writing dialogue over an ongoing firefight. Dunmore addresses the new variable in his calculation. Nell watches his hands. Abel's attention keeps moving between the brake and the slope.

Use the existing rescue, evasion, exploitation, betrayal and missed outcomes. A successful threat does not become a lengthy negotiation that overrides those rules. The companion dialogue offers replacements within the existing meanings.

Proposed visual distinction: Nell remains beside her stock when rescued; she leaves with little when robbed. Abel's survival matters through ordinary work later, not a sentimental farewell inserted before every possible death.

Exit: the player can continue west by every supported outcome. A player who missed the encounter must never be told they watched it.

### Gate and clinic

Talbot gives a short instruction about weapons and points to the sheriff. If the player pauses, he offers a practical answer about where to find help. He need not demand a biography.

Proposed optional clinic scene after a bad caravan outcome: Keene asks Talbot for clean cloth. He brings the entire bundle; she separates what is usable and returns the rest. He is eager, inexperienced and willing to learn. The stranger can leave without being forced to watch.

Nell refuses direct conversation after exploitation, as already designed. Do not weaken that refusal by giving her an explanatory monologue through the player. Keene can state the necessary medical boundary without speaking for Nell's forgiveness.

### Sheriff and the difference between work and absolution

Pritchard accepts the dispatch and records the known caravan outcome. Keep each existing debrief path. Silence advances the hand-off without becoming an admission. The existing chapter hook remains available even after an ugly outcome.

The proposed performance separates two things: he needs more people than he has, and he has not settled his opinion of this one. Offer useful work without ceremonially deputizing or forgiving the stranger.

Proposed optional beat: after rescue, he sets a second cup within reach and continues sorting papers. After exploitation, he leaves the cup where it is. No close-up or explanatory line.

### Signed Clean and the first return visit

All existing dispositions remain: report, publish without a name, name the yard on sufficient evidence, scapegoat Haskell, sell, burn or retain. No new clue secretly makes Haskell culpable.

The strongest emotional revision is a future scene for the scapegoat branch. The player returns to find the receiving desk staffed badly or left unattended. The empty work surface communicates what the town lost before anyone comments. A later correction must restore public accuracy and practical opportunity; it cannot purchase Haskell's affection.

That later repair quest is proposed content, not an extension of the current slice's stage logic.

## Level two Chapter 2 scenes

### Shared structure and order independence

Each destination has a local cause, someone defending a harmful expedient, and a resolution that leaves a visible cost. Any destination can be first. Each yields a lead toward a common freight transaction, while the other two enrich the case. Do not require all three local problems to be solved before the player can follow the main lead.

Proposed main-route fallback: the sheriff receives a duplicate freight inquiry after the first destination returns a report. If the player destroys or sells local papers, the inquiry provides access to the next chapter without recreating optional evidence or granting an accusation. This needs a future quest design before implementation.

New people are identified by working roles below. Proper names, appearances and affiliations remain open.

### Iron Junction and the late inspection

Roles: an older yard supervisor and an apprentice fitter. Neither is declared responsible for forging Ostrander's name.

Scene one: the apprentice measures a condemned component while the supervisor tells a crew which wagon can move. There is competence here alongside the failure. The player can request records, compare components or leave. With YardAlerted, the supervisor knows an inquiry is coming and insists on witnesses; that is wariness, not proof of guilt.

Scene two: proposed discovery of a separate authorization to defer repairs. The supervisor signed it to keep a medicine shipment moving during an earlier shortage, then allowed the temporary exception to persist. This is a specific admitted act. It does not establish who forged the inspection ledger or ordered sabotage.

Scene three: a work stoppage requires a decision about scarce replacement stock. The player may support keeping the affected section closed, arrange voluntary temporary labor and supplies, or accept restricted operation with the defect disclosed. Restricted operation is permissible only if a later technical design establishes a defensible safety limit. No dialogue check makes unsafe metal safe.

Consequences: closure delays food; paid repair consumes resources; supervised operation consumes staff and still limits capacity. There is no option labeled simply Save the Railway.

Supervisor arc: first disputes the scope of the accusation; later signs an accurate defect notice; finally accepts removal from sole approval authority. If the player exposes the supervisor publicly, the repair can still proceed under another worker. The injured community decides whether to employ the supervisor afterward. Confession alone earns no redemption.

### Greenwater and the undelivered grain

Roles: a ration custodian and a young river porter. The porter is an adult with little bargaining power; exact age is open.

Scene one: the store has grain on paper, little on its shelves and a cooking queue. The custodian is reducing portions but has not falsified household counts. Avoid turning every administrator into the same dishonest character.

Scene two: grain waits at a transfer point because landing damage, disputed freight charges and a privately purchased hold have compounded. The porter quietly moved some sacks to a dry loft, breaking handling rules to prevent spoilage. The saved sacks are evidence of an unauthorized action and useful food; neither fact erases the other.

Scene three: the player can organize an immediate smaller delivery, negotiate release against a recorded debt, or secure the transfer point for slower bulk movement. The custodian refuses to promise equal portions if the supply cannot support them. A truthful ration board may provoke anger without being a bad outcome.

Consequences: fast delivery leaves some grain at risk; debt creates a future obligation; bulk movement leaves the queue waiting longer. Local supplies and porter testimony can identify a paid hold, but cannot by themselves prove the payer intended starvation.

Return image: the queue changes length and the porter can stand openly at the unloading point if protected by the settlement. Poverty must not make the porter either automatically saintly or automatically dishonest.

### Red Hollow and the sealed mine

Roles: a veteran pump keeper and a mine delegate. The pump keeper has bodily limits and deep practical knowledge; the delegate has families depending on wages.

Scene one: the seal is maintained by workers who fear another failure, rather than a mysterious authority with no visible people. The delegate offers to accompany the player because the stoppage is also threatening winter stores.

Scene two: water marks, maintenance tags and testimony establish a real pumping problem. Proposed evidence also shows that someone bought up replacement parts through an intermediary. The safety closure predates that intervention; the financier has worsened an existing crisis.

Scene three: negotiate an aboveground repair and supply exchange; help recover accessible equipment without reopening production; or postpone repair and arrange temporary work elsewhere. Entering dangerous workings is optional and cannot be the only solution.

Consequences: production stays low during repair; salvage limits later capacity; temporary work separates households and makes return uncertain. Nobody wins an argument by belittling frightened workers.

Return image: a repaired pump can be heard before ore moves. A council notice names who checked it and who may stop it again. This is an original proposed practice, not a claim about historical mining procedure.

## Level two Chapter 3 scenes

### Offers at the freight table

Proposed venue: a freight shelter near Mercy Crossing, using a familiar space before introducing another large hub.

Four negotiating roles represent the four existing principal factions: Compact freight negotiator, Commonwealth provisioning delegate, Dominion escort officer and Ascendant field technician. They are representatives, not necessarily faction leaders. The Ascendant's public presence, insignia and knowledge require owner review because the act's concealment rules and chapter title are not yet reconciled.

Until that review, keep the scene in planning form. No technical or political representative names The Deep or explains MERIDIAN to townsfolk.

Each offer answers a genuine need:

- Compact: shared freight with independent operators; small carriers fear dependence on the largest buyer.
- Commonwealth: pooled food and labor; households want to know who has access to the stores and who can challenge an allocation.
- Dominion: escorts and enforceable road rules; towns object to searches and jurisdiction that outlast the emergency.
- Ascendants: useful technical repair; locals ask who controls access afterward and whether outsiders can inspect the work.

The wanderer can inspect terms, carry a counteroffer, decline to endorse anyone or leave. No binding principal allegiance is required here.

### The shot and the witnesses

The established chapter beat is the assassination of a messenger carrying mountain intelligence, associated with a distinctive silver cartridge.

Proposed staging: the messenger arrives while crews are unloading, not during a four-faction villain roll call. After the shot, each faction has someone doing practical emergency work. Suspicion crosses useful cooperation.

The player may help the wounded, protect the case, follow the firing position or preserve witness accounts. Missing one action does not end the chapter. A duplicate delivery instruction provides the main lead if the case is lost. It contains routing information, not a replacement for every piece of evidence.

Keep cartridge appearance distinct from an accusation. An examiner can identify the observed feature; authorial knowledge that Cassian Voss is a silver-gunned assassin is not knowledge every local possesses. No compulsory deduction names him prematurely.

### Aftermath

A claim of emergency authority becomes tempting precisely because somebody was killed. The player can support a narrow search, help witnesses leave safely or insist on recording the scope of a search. Each action affects who will testify later. Failure to cooperate is not proof of conspiracy.

The scene should make it possible to dislike a representative and still value the people they brought.

## Level two Chapter 4 scenes

### Getting to Vane without Signed Clean

Proposed independent lead: a carrier has duplicate fee receipts for a shipment that never moved. Corroboration from a transfer record or voluntary testimony establishes that money bought a delay. Vane's business mark connects a transaction to his books, not automatically to every regional disaster.

Access routes: ask him to reconcile a documented dispute; obtain a copy through the carrier; or enter the accounts room without permission. Trespass may provide evidence but should not be the only route. No lockpicking skill or earlier sale of the ledger gates the chapter.

### The accounting confrontation

The player assembles three distinct propositions: payment occurred; a contracted delay occurred; the delay served Vane's interest in preserving isolated markets. The books and testimony must support each claim before dialogue treats it as proved.

A separate column records legitimate aid and freight expenses. These are real transactions, not fake evidence inserted to excuse him. He has helped people and used dependence to keep influence. The tension comes from those facts coexisting.

Proposed motive: he believes integrated routes will give larger outside powers control of local trade. He has made himself the person allowed to decide how much hardship others should bear to prevent that. This is a defense he offers, not the story's verdict.

### Three resolutions with specific costs

Expose: release a bounded, evidenced account. Proposed bridge financing from willing carriers can keep some freight moving, but service contracts remain disrupted. Do not make exposure kill patients as automatic punishment for honesty.

Pressure: require release of holds and independent review of future fees in exchange for delaying public disclosure. Immediate flow improves; people whose losses remain undisclosed may later object. The player must actually maintain the condition or accept its failure.

Recruit: retain his logistics skill under visible restrictions, restitution and an independent counter-signature. He does not become a loyal friend because a persuasion check succeeded. Refusal to accept oversight leaves exposure or disengagement available.

Main-route fallback: a duplicated freight authorization points to Blackstone regardless of whether Vane cooperates or survives. Local judgments vary; the next chapter remains reachable.

### Return consequences

Nell measures the resolution by whether carriers can refuse terms. Haskell, if falsely accused, wants the correction posted where the accusation was posted. Pritchard must accept that his own administrative choices can also be examined.

A good settlement restores an activity and allocates responsibility. It does not require everyone to agree that the player is good.

## Level two Chapter 5 boundary

Prepare Blackstone's public platform, freight stairs, service access and sightlines as proposed places. Offer an overt approach, observation from cover and a route out. Evidence of recent passage can invite investigation without assigning the player a remembered gesture or involuntary emotional response.

Mara's encounter, exact knowledge, prior actions, proof of Rider identity and family revelation remain outline anchors from the existing spine. Do not write their detailed exchanges until the owner settles the linked history questions.

No silent-protagonist cinematic should make the wanderer embrace Mara, recognize a parent or claim an inherited right without player choice.
