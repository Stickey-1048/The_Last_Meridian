## Required narrow clauses

1. **Terminal rescue selection must not preempt a pending break plan.** The contract defines exclusive terminal MineOutcome values, stage57 for an unpledged/unblasted rescue, and stage52 only after rescuing everyone and subsequently operating the inner-break control. At the intermediate instant when all eleven reach the surface, neither the blast nor a pledge may exist. An automatic57 at that instant would lock out the explicitly intended52 path. Specify: physical arrival sets cohort facts and `shift_saved`, not automatically a terminal outcome. A pending BreakPlan keeps the outcome unresolved and permits only its safe completion or explicit abandonment. Commit51 after its actual undertaking/rescue requirements; commit52 after the observed post-rescue collapse; commit57 only upon an explicit leave-break/abandon-plan decision. Do not switch an already committed terminal outcome silently. Choosing no pledge is not by itself the terminal57 trigger before a pending blast is resolved.

2. **Rescue readiness must accommodate the actual-casualty adapter.** The eleven-person section says the batch requires "all eleven must be ready," whereas stage58 intentionally permits observed deaths followed by rescue of all survivors. An unconditional all-eleven-alive/ready gate would make58 unreachable after a real casualty. Specify the batch roster as every surviving assigned cohort member not yet verified safe; retain known observed deaths separately and never move/resurrect/replace them. For an ordinary full rescue, eleven distinct living arrivals remain mandatory. For58, all eleven identities must have known actual fates and every survivor must be verified safe, with no missing/disabled/unobserved actor inferred dead. Zero survivors requires eleven observed deaths, not a zero-length-roster shortcut. Stage58 must not set `shift_saved`,51,52 or an all-eleven speech.

3. **Adjournment is not proof that both dockets were referred.** Accepted `quests/tlm_mq02c.json` defines `quill_shamed` as presentation, adjournment **and both dockets sent for review**. The proposed stage13 trigger establishes living custody/adjournment but does not yet specify the referral acknowledgment. Keep13 truthful for the established hearing/custody facts; persist referral pending separately, and set the full canonical flag only after an actual represented referral/recording acknowledgment. Do not invent a new letter or clerk, and do not infer the referral from held docket or a click alone. The existing evidence/court record and technical status can represent the narrow referral action. Recheck living Quill, the current prisoner, actual copy possession and current court outcome before its commit; cancellation or stale context grants no referral flag.
