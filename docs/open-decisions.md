# Open Decisions and Continuity Conflicts

1. **The Deep governance:** Older story says Helena Sorrell's Continuity Directorate runs The Deep; new four-faction canon makes People's Commonwealth a principal power, and Ascendant Directorate a different principal power. Choose whether The Deep belongs to the Commonwealth, a now-collapsed Continuity institution, or a formally independent city. Do not introduce a fifth endgame faction by accident.
2. **Rusk vs Rook:** Gideon Rusk leads Iron Dominion. Owen Pritchard is Mercy Crossing sheriff/Old Warden. Keep distinct.
3. **Clara Whitcomb vs Cassian Voss:** Same surname may imply kinship; otherwise rename one to avoid confusing accidental implication.
4. **Malcolm Ash vs Gideon Rusk:** Distinct ghoul railway veteran and Iron Dominion marshal; shared first name could be revised for clarity.
5. **Mara Vale vs Dorran Coyle:** Shared surname requires planned connection or revision.
6. **Hollowborn anatomy:** Set strict biological design bounds; antler-like growths and fox cues must remain subtle human traits.
7. **Fallout 76 source audit:** Initial Bethesda-source audit is recorded in docs/fallout-lore-audit.md. Quest-level terminal/dialogue audit still required before final canon lock.
8. **Transport:** Determine operational limits for train, horses and river craft within a manageable set of exterior worldspaces.
9. **Quest estimates:** 17 main chapters, ~45 side/faction quests, ~14 companion loyalty quests are targets, not commitments until scoped.
10. **Ending mechanics:** Define precise alliances/hostilities, diplomacy exceptions and consequences; ensure no 100% perfect route without tradeoffs.
11. **Player identity:** Fifth Rider background must be compatible with customizable character creation and player-agency choices.
12. **Mara Vale:** Define what she knew during player self-erasure and what she did in her morally fraught past.
13. **Art and asset rights:** Original imagery and models only; don't redistribute proprietary Fallout or other franchise assets without authorization.
