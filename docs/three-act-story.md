# Main Story Spine — 17 Chapters
Status: PROVISIONAL quest beats beneath locked act structure. All geography is southern Appalachia.

## Act I — The Broken Roads
**01 Six Rounds to Mercy** — Play as a surface-born descendant of Deep exiles, unaware of family origins. Begin at a wrecked train in the Tellico valley. Scavenge revolver and dispatch; help, evade or exploit a threatened caravan; reach Mercy Crossing and Sheriff Owen Pritchard. Tutorial for combat, dialogue and scavenging.
**02 Three Roads to Ruin** — Investigate Greenwater's missing food, Red Hollow's sealed mine, and Iron Junction's sabotaged railway in any order. Teach routes by road, rail and river.
**03 The Four Flags** — Regional leaders contest security, property and resources. A messenger bearing mountain intelligence is assassinated with a distinctive silver cartridge.
**04 The Black Ledger** — Merchant Silas Vane's books reveal financing intended to preserve local isolation; Vane may be exposed, pressured or recruited.
**05 The Fifth Rider** — Confrontation at Blackstone Station; meet Mara Vale; recover proof the player was a Meridian Rider; Mara reveals the player's family came from The Deep, and the player enters the hidden transit/elevator network.

## Act II — The Hidden Kingdoms
**06 A Thousand Floors** — Enter the protagonist's ancestral home, The Deep, and confront a sealed family record, communal history and hidden civil factions. The Deep's administration must be reconciled with the later People's Commonwealth: see open decisions; do not presume old Continuity Directorate remains a fifth political power.
**07 The City That Never Was** — Reach Haven Nine dissidents; find erased records proving social manipulation.
**08 The Iron Saint** — Secure passage through Appalachian rail corridors. Restore the nuclear locomotive in optional stages; alternatives must exist to prevent side-quest gating.
**09 The Shrouded Divide** — Encounter Hollowborn political societies and competing origin claims. Negotiate passage without completing every regional faction arc.
**10 The Oak Ridge Files** — Examine fictional secret experiments associated with Oak Ridge, unethical research and the Distant Signal.
**11 The Unwritten History** — Recover targeted recent memories of the Fifth Rider mission (not the player's entire identity), the exile record and Mara's role; failure of The Deep's sealed systems produces a refugee crisis. Broadcast: 'The isolation phase is complete.'

## Act III — The Last Meridian
**12 The Open Frontier** — Manage refugees, food, security and local councils; meet four principal claimants to regional authority.
**13 The Silent War** — MERIDIAN disrupts power, water, traffic and automated plants, tempting populations to accept centralized control.
**14 The Last Riders** — Confront Cassian Voss, Lucien Grey and Edda Flint; resolve loyalty, betrayal, duels and information.
**15 The Gathering** — Commit to the Free Meridian Compact, People's Commonwealth, Iron Dominion or Ascendant Directorate; negotiate The Deep's independent, allied, integrated or ascendant role, with conditional ancillary support.
**16 The Siege of Meridian** — Reach Meridian Prime with faction-specific infiltration, military, technological or diplomatic plans.
**17 The Last Decision** — Destroy, partition, subject to oversight or integrate MERIDIAN and settle the fate of the player's ancestral city. Faction governance plus companion and regional epilogues; no ending should erase player consequences.

## Quest implementation invariants
- No uncompleted ancillary quest blocks progress to main quest 17.
- Player can reach Act III without having chosen a main faction earlier.
- One shared climactic worldspace, reusing content with divergent quest stages.
- Faction endings differ in government, civil rights, technology, conflict and economic arrangements.
- Every quest needs a stage table, aliases, prerequisites, fallback conditions, dialogue and game-test checklist before coding.
