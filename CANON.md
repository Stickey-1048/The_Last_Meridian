# THE LAST MERIDIAN — Canon Registry
Version: 1.0-draft | Status: Foundation pending owner approval | Date: 2026-11-11

## Creative identity [LOCKED]
A Fallout 4 total-conversion fan-mod concept set in **2317** in the southern Appalachians: eastern Tennessee, western North Carolina and northern Georgia. It is an Appalachian **gunslinger Western**, not a desert Western. Lawlessness, frontier justice, mountain clans, trade, rail marshals, isolated towns and hard travel provide the Western atmosphere. The central conflict is who should govern recovered infrastructure and whether anyone should surrender agency to MERIDIAN's predictions.

## Geography [LOCKED: region; PROVISIONAL: exact map placement]
- Tellico River / Tellico Plains-inspired **Broken Valleys**: Mercy Crossing opening hub.
- Loudon / Lenoir City-inspired **Iron Corridor**: Iron Junction, railway yards and foundries.
- Great Smokies / Nantahala-inspired **Shrouded Divide**: Hollowborn towns.
- North Georgia Blue Ridge-inspired **Cinder Highlands**: mining, outlaws, ghoul settlements.
- **Oak Ridge Exclusion**: fictional extensions of a real historical research center.
- **The Deep** and **Meridian Prime**: underground fictional facilities in the eastern Tennessee region.
- Transportation by mountain highways, surviving railways, riverboats and reservoirs. No Arizona, deserts, western Tennessee, or transcontinental journey.

## Structure [LOCKED]
Act I **The Broken Roads**: local survival and gunslinger tutorials; road/rail sabotage; ends with encounter with Mara Vale and discovery of underground facilities.
Act II **The Hidden Kingdoms**: Mara (narrative co-protagonist), The Deep, hidden research, Appalachia, Hollowborn, Oak Ridge, and the player's erased role as the Fifth Rider.
Act III **The Last Meridian**: choose primary allegiance, make conditional alliances, decide the fate of the MERIDIAN intelligence at Meridian Prime.
Core spine: 17 main chapters; target 30–45 hours, plus substantial optional content. All targets are aspirational.

## Exactly four principal endgame factions [LOCKED]
1. **Free Meridian Compact**: free trade, voluntary association, loose democratic councils, local self-determination; tension between fair rules and merchant monopolies; may cooperate conditionally with several rivals. Leader **Clara Whitcomb**.
2. **People's Commonwealth**: initially survival-driven communal ownership and egalitarian ideals, corrupted across generations into a privileged party hierarchy; possibility of reform, though still an ideologically distinct path. Leader **First Secretary Anton Vey**.
3. **Iron Dominion**: militarized offshoot of the Wardens; territorial domination, coercive unity, seeks to destroy Commonwealth and Ascendant ruling structures, may subordinate a compliant Compact; internal Old Wardens reformists. Leader **Marshal Gideon Rusk**, hardliner **Colonel Amos Brigg**.
4. **Ascendant Directorate**: technocratic governance through predictive systems, scientific dominance, infrastructure control; debate between transparency and Convergence. Leader **Dr. Adrian Kess**, field operative **Lucien Grey**, reformer **Dr. Celia Marr**.

These political positions are fictional and should have credible advocates, internal inconsistencies, dissenters, tradeoffs, and actual human costs. Avoid simplistic claims that every adherent of an ideology is malicious.

## Exactly four ancillary power blocs [LOCKED]
1. **Oak Ridge Scientific Assembly** — leader **Evelyn Harrow**; Open Science **Naomi Bell**, Human Advancement **Julian Wren**.
2. **Concord of the Hollows** — heterogeneous mountain alliance; diplomacy, sovereignty, watershed rights; council speaker **Seren Ashfall** [PROVISIONAL].
3. **Ashen Brotherhood** — non-feral ghoul society at Cinder Station; leader **Malcolm Ash**, militant internal faction under **Miriam Kade**.
4. **Stonebound Houses** — mining and engineering culture; Karn Veyr ancestral city; claimant **Dorran Coyle**.

Other organizations (Brass Consortium, Keepers of the Ash, Ridgeborn Covenant, Wardens/Old Wardens, Black Rail Syndicate) exist as internal caucuses or localized groups, NOT additional principal endgame powers.

## Hollowborn [LOCKED: concept, PROVISIONAL: origins]
**Hollowborn** is an umbrella designation for recognizably human populations displaying subtly altered inherited features. **Glassborn is one subtype**, not a synonym. Groups include Hartfolk, Mirekin, Ursans, Nocturnes, Vulpers, Glassborn, Eldren; the Stonebound represent a distinct intertwined culture and possible lineage, and the Forged are later engineered descendants. No literal magical elves/dwarves/orcs. Roots may link to fictional classified Oak Ridge research beginning with Manhattan Project-era observations, later postwar experiments, and/or preexisting populations and a puzzling signal. Do not canonically settle the origin in advance.

## Antagonistic mystery [LOCKED]
MERIDIAN is an invented pre-war continuity / infrastructure intelligence compromised or guided by a **Distant Signal** of uncertain origin. This is NOT an explanation of the Great War or all Fallout events. Five Riders: player (the Fifth Rider, customizable identity), **Mara Vale** (deserter), **Cassian Voss** (silver-gunned assassin), **Edda Flint** (independent), **Lucien Grey** (signal aligned). The player voluntarily erased memories to hide information. Mara is a major story protagonist/companion introduced at the end of Act I.

## Side-content principle [LOCKED]
Ancillary faction quests and local settlement arcs can offer allies, trade, equipment, reputation and regional epilogues, but must never arbitrarily gate Acts II–III. Main-faction allegiances primarily commit in Act III. The climactic location is shared, with different aims, allies, approaches and endings.
